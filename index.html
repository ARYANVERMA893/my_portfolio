05 / Publications

Writing & research.

Robotics / Industrial Safety
Replacing Human Workers with Robots in Hazardous and Deadly Chemical Factory Environments

The dangerous chemical factory environment poses risks of toxic exposure, extreme thermal conditions, volatile reactions, and invisible airborne hazards to human labor. This paper describes an AI-based robotic system framework to replace human labor in high-risk industrial environments by facilitating safe and reliable autonomous operations under highly constrained and unpredictable conditions. A real-time sensor fusion system with vision, gas, and proximity sensors improves situational awareness to monitor toxic leaks and dynamic hazards, enabling adaptive, safe autonomous operation.

LLMs / Real-Time Systems
Smart Disaster Alert System Powered by Retrieval-Augmented Large Language Models and Real-Time Intelligence Pipelines

This paper proposes a Smart Disaster Alert System that incorporates Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and Large Language Model (LLM) design to provide real-time detection and notification of disasters. The framework uses Apache Kafka, PySpark, and AWS S3 for real-time data processing. Integrating RAG search with LLM summarisation enables extraction of situational insight and delivery of context-aware alerts, achieving a 40% reduction in response time.
